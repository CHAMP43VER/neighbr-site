What you can do

Everything your block has to offer

One feed for the whole neighborhood: the person who bakes, the kid who mows, the couch that needs a new home, and the party on Saturday.

Buy and sell nearby

Services, homemade food, and secondhand finds from real neighbors. Browse what's around you, or explore the whole map.

Book a neighbor for the job

Lawn care, car detailing, tutoring, a hand moving furniture. Pick a time slot, pay in the app, and the money is released when the job is done.

Homemade food

Sourdough, tamales, birthday cakes, weeknight dinners. Order from the neighbor who cooks, and pick it up down the street.

Neighborhood events

Garage sales, park cleanups, birthday parties. Post one in seconds, see who's interested, and message your guest list.

The Neighbors map

A live, privacy-first map of neighbors using the app near you, with approximate locations only, and going invisible is one switch.

Safe by design

Payments held securely until the job's done, in-app messaging with contact protection, and block & report tools everywhere.

A closer look at the app

Buying

From the feed to your door in two taps

Every listing shows the price, the distance, and the seller's profile and reviews. Tap Buy, pay inside the app, and sort out pickup in chat.

  • Distance and seller profile on every listing
  • Reviews from past buyers
  • Message the seller without sharing your number

Selling

Post a listing in under a minute

Pick what you are selling, add a photo and a price, and it is live for the people around you. Services get weekly hours so neighbors can book a slot.

  • Services, food, and products in one flow
  • Payouts straight to your bank through Stripe
  • Boost a listing when you want more eyes on it

How it works

Up and running in three steps

No forms, no waiting list. You can be browsing your block before your coffee cools.

  1. 1

    Download and sign in

    Get Neighbr from the App Store and sign in with Apple. Add a name and a photo so neighbors know who they are dealing with.

  2. 2

    Set your neighborhood

    Share your location or enter a ZIP code. Your feed fills with what is close, and your own posts show an approximate location only.

  3. 3

    Buy, book, or post

    Grab something from the feed, book a time with a neighbor, or list what you have. Chat and payment stay inside the app.
