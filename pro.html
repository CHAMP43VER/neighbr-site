Common questions

How do I subscribe from here?

Checkout on this page is tied to your Neighbr account, so it starts from inside the app: open Neighbr, go to Settings, then Neighbr Pro. If you landed here on your own, the button under the price points you back to the app.

I already pay for Pro through the App Store. What now?

Cancel that subscription in your Apple account settings first, so you are not paying twice. Once it ends, subscribe here at the lower price.

How do I cancel?

From Settings, then Neighbr Pro, in the app. Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.
